# 360\_Bangkok

This repository contains data and tools for the 360\_Bangkok project, which captures panoramic street-view video from a motorcyclist's perspective across Bangkok.

---

## Overview

Between **December 18, 2022**, and **January 16, 2023**, we recorded routine journeys on a motorcycle between Phaya Thai district and Chulalongkorn University (central Bangkok) using a GoPro Max camera in 360° video mode (5.6K resolution, 30 fps). These trips, taken at various times (peak and off-peak, weekdays and weekends), pose no additional risk to the rider.

The resulting dataset provides a full 360° view of Bangkok’s street scenes from a helmet-mounted perspective. Example locations are marked with green dots on the map below, and sample frames (left) alongside object-detection outputs (right) are shown in Figure 1.

## Dataset

* **Source**: 360° videos uploaded to Mapillary.
* **Access**: Use the provided Jupyter notebook (`360_Bangkok.ipynb`) to download and extract frame images from the Mapillary uploads.
* **Request Videos**: Contact the me to request the raw 360° video files.


## Ethics and Approvals

* **Ethics approval**: University College London & Chulalongkorn University.
* **Participant safety**: Journeys represented routine travel and did not increase risk to the rider.
